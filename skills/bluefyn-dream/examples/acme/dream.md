# Dream: Acme Pay

Three concepts for the Acme Pay content. Acme Pay is a made-up company.

## The page has to make one reader do one thing

Reader: the finance lead at a payments company who reviews vendor invoices by hand.
The one thing: book the discovery call.

## Concept a: Spine

- The idea: a navy spine holds the proof and the next step, and the argument runs down the page beside it.
- Hero: `stream`, the record texture run in vertical columns, rasterized and set behind the rail.
- Structure: a 196pt rail on the left from top to bottom, holding the lockup, the three figures and the
  next step with the contact. The headline and every other section stack in one column on the right.
- Ground: paper body and navy rail in light; night body and navy rail in dark. The layer card is night in
  light mode and charcoal with a hairline in dark mode.
- Type: a 25pt headline; the figures at 27pt in the rail.
- Remembered for: the spine. The next step is always visible at the bottom left.

## Concept b: Ledger

- The idea: Swiss grid and large type on one ground, with a block of slanted evidence cut into the corner.
- Hero: `slanted_field`, rasterized as a dark block in the top-right corner, bleeding off the top and the
  right edge.
- Structure: twelve 34pt columns. The headline and lede run across eight columns, and the figures hang
  under the block like a ledger. "Every bill" is set at 46pt, the largest type on the page. The layer
  stack is set as type rows, with Bluefyn as the one blue row. The page has no bands and no cards.
- Ground: white in light mode, ink in dark mode. The block uses charcoal and gray digits in dark mode.
- Type: a 30pt headline; heavy 1.5pt rules start each section.
- Remembered for: the giant answer line and the black corner.

## Concept c: Tower

- The idea: the finished tower pattern rises out of the page, and the problem and the answer read across
  one row below it.
- Hero: `patterns`, the design team's tower pattern on paper in light mode and on night in dark mode, set
  on the same ground as the page so the tower has no frame.
- Structure: a 272pt poster top with the copy on the left and the tower at the right edge. Below it, one
  row holds the three figures and then the answer in blue, so the page reads problem to answer. A Bluefyn
  blue band closes the page with the next step.
- Ground: paper in light mode, night in dark mode, blue for the footer in both.
- Type: a 26pt headline; the answer figure is in blue.
- Remembered for: the tower, and the blue band at the bottom.

## Why these are three different designs

Spine is vertical: a rail and a column. Ledger has no blocks of color and leads with type on a strict
grid. Tower is a poster with a picture on top and a band at the bottom. Each uses a different hero family:
a texture, a drawing and a finished brand file. None of them uses the template's navy header.
