# The Bluefyn voice

Bluefyn checks every fee a company pays and every invoice it sends against its contracts. Its readers are
finance leads, controllers and operators at payments and software companies. They are busy, they know the
terms, and they distrust claims they cannot check.

So the copy is plain, specific and calm. It reads like a careful colleague explaining what will happen.

## Rules

1. **Lead with the point.** The first sentence says what changes for the reader.
2. **Be specific.** Name the document, the check, the number. "Every vendor invoice against its contract"
   beats "your financial workflows".
3. **Plain words.** Use, help, show, get, many. Not leverage, facilitate, showcase, garner, numerous.
4. **Short sentences.** One idea each. Split a sentence that makes the reader go back.
5. **Active voice.** "Bluefyn flags the overcharge", not "overcharges are flagged".
6. **No hype.** No revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changing, best-in-class, unlock, empower.
   If it is good, the fact shows it.
7. **Calm punctuation.** Periods and commas. No em dashes, en dashes, arrows, exclamation points, emoji or
   ellipsis characters. Straight quotes. Colons only before a list.
8. **Sentence case.** Headings and labels capitalize the first word and proper nouns only.
9. **No filler.** Cut "in order to", "it is important to note", "at the end of the day", "feel free to".
10. **Only true claims.** Every figure, name and result comes from the source. Write "about 300" if the
    source says "~300". Never round a number up to make it sound bigger.

## Before and after

**Headline**

- Before: "Revolutionize Your Finance Operations With AI-Powered Contract Intelligence!"
- After: "Every vendor and customer contract, owned, checked and billed correctly."

**Lede**

- Before: "In today's fast-paced landscape, finance teams face mounting pressure. Our cutting-edge platform
  seamlessly empowers you to unlock hidden savings."
- After: "Acme Pay runs on a stack of processors, banks and identity vendors, each on its own contract.
  Bluefyn checks every bill from them against those contracts, so Acme pays what it agreed to."

**Proof point**

- Before: "Massive overbilling — $120,000!!"
- After: figure "$120K", text "overbilled by one vendor in a single quarter".

**List item**

- Before: "Real-time monitoring → instant alerts 🚨."
- After: "An alert before a volume shift moves you across a tier"

**Next step**

- Before: "Feel free to reach out anytime to learn more about how we can help!"
- After: "A 30-minute call this week to map your vendor contracts. Then we start checking."

**Email**

- Before: "Hi Sam — hope you're well! Just circling back to see if you had a chance to look at the deck. We're
  super excited about the opportunity to partner and would love to find time to connect!"
- After: "Hi Sam, did you get a chance to read the deck? If the fee review is still a priority, I can walk
  your team through it on Thursday or Friday. Which suits you?"
